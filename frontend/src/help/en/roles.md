Traced has three roles. The owner can do everything, a manager runs the day-to-day work, and a worker scans in the warehouse. You add people and set their role in **Settings › Users**.

## Owner

Everything a manager can do, plus the account-level settings:

- Connect or disconnect Shopify and Bosta, and switch on other couriers.
- Change the business details and the Pick & Pack mode.
- See **Analytics** and Shopify customer data requests (**Privacy**).
- Give someone the owner role — only the current owner can.

## Manager

- Uses every warehouse screen: **Orders**, **Inventory**, **Receiving**, **Stock take**, **Transfers**, **Alerts**, return and exchange requests, and all the worker screens.
- Records refunds and handles the returns portal settings.
- Opens **Settings**, but sees the business details, connections, Pick & Pack mode and courier settings read-only.
- Adds, edits and deactivates workers and managers — never the owner.

## Worker

- Signs in at a shared warehouse tablet with their name and a 4-digit PIN — no email or password needed. See [Set up the warehouse tablet](/help/tablet-setup).
- Lands on a home screen with their tasks: **Pick & Pack**, **Scan returns**, **Pickups**, **Lookup**, and putting pieces on shelves.
- Doesn't see Settings, Orders, Inventory, Analytics or refunds.

## Adding someone

1. Go to **Settings › Users** and tap **Add user**.
2. Enter a name and choose the role. Owners and managers sign in with an email and a password (at least 8 characters); workers get a 4-digit PIN.
3. Tap **Create user**.

To change a person's role later, tap **Edit**. Someone who leaves can be deactivated: they can't sign in any more, but everything they did stays in the history, and an owner or manager can reactivate them.
