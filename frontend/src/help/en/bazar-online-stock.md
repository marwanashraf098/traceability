**Remove from online stock** takes the pieces off your Shopify stock when you mark them as sent, so they can't sell online while they're away. **Keep showing online** leaves your Shopify stock as it is; each piece sold at the bazar comes off afterwards.

## Remove from online stock (the default)
- Nothing changes in Shopify when you create the bazar or scan pieces out.
- When you press **Mark as sent**, Traced lowers your stock at the Traced Main location in Shopify by the pieces that left your main warehouse.
- Sales at the bazar change nothing more in Shopify: those pieces already came off.
- After the bazar ends, each good leftover goes back on your Shopify stock when it is scanned back (or brought back by count) at the main warehouse.
- Damaged and lost pieces don't go back.

## Keep showing online
- Nothing changes in Shopify when the stock is sent. Online customers still see these pieces.
- When a piece sells at the bazar, Shopify updates usually within a few minutes after the sale. An undone sale, a return or a piece marked lost updates Shopify the same way.
- **The last piece can sell online and at the bazar.** Both see it as available until the bazar sale reaches Shopify. **Tip:** use Keep for stock you have plenty of.

## Changing the choice
The choice belongs to the location: every bazar held there uses it. Change it on the bazar's page from the **Online stock** row › **Change**, until you mark the stock as sent. After that it is fixed for this bazar.

Traced only ever changes the Traced Main location in Shopify — never your other Shopify locations.
