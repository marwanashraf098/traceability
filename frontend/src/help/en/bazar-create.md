Go to **Bazar** › **New bazar**, fill in the name, place, days, how customers pay and who can sell, then press **Create · scan pieces out**. The bazar opens on its Get ready page.

## Name & place
- Type the bazar's name. It is for you and your team — customers don't see it.
- Choose **New bazar location** and name the venue, or **A location I already have** for a venue you have used before.

## When
Pick the first selling day, then the last. Each day gets its own cash close.

## How customers pay
Tick **Cash**, **InstaPay** and **Card** — any of them. Only the ones you tick show on the sell screen, and each one is checked when you close a day.

## Who can sell
Tick **Owners**, **Managers** and **Workers**. Only the roles you tick can sell at this bazar — owners and managers included. Sellers you invite join as workers, so tick **Workers** if you will invite anyone.

## Online stock
Choose **Remove from online stock** (the default) or **Keep showing online**. The choice belongs to the location: every bazar held there uses it. See [Online stock: Remove or Keep](/help/bazar-online-stock).

## Next
Press **Create · scan pieces out**. The bazar opens on its Get ready page, where you scan pieces out, set prices and start — see [Get ready, send and start](/help/bazar-get-ready).

**Tip:** bringing stock that isn't in Traced yet? Use **Set up from new stock** on the Bazar page instead — see [Set up a bazar from new stock](/help/bazar-setup-new-stock).
