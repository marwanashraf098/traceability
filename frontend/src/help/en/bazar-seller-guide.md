Open the link the owner sent you on WhatsApp, tap **Start selling** and pick a 4-digit PIN. Then scan a piece's label to sell it.

## Get in
1. Open the link on your phone and tap **Start selling**.
2. Type a 4-digit **PIN**, then **PIN again**, and tap **Save PIN and start selling**.
3. Next time, just open Traced on the same phone: the live bazar opens first.

The link works once, within 24 hours. You need your PIN when you switch to yourself on a shared phone (menu › **Switch seller**).

## Sell one piece
1. Choose **Quick · scan = sold**.
2. Point the camera at one label. Each scan is one sale.
3. The green card shows **SOLD**. Tap **Cash**, **InstaPay** or **Card** to change how the customer paid, and add their name and phone if they want.

Made a mistake? Tap **Undo** on the card. You can still undo your own sale for 2 minutes: in **Earlier today**, tap the sale, then **Undo this sale**. After that, ask the owner or a manager.

## A cart
1. Choose **Cart** and scan each piece.
2. Add a **Discount** if you give one — whole pounds, for the whole cart.
3. Choose how the customer paid, add their name and phone if they want, and tap **Charge**.

A cart holds up to 50 pieces.

## A piece without a label
Tap **No barcode? Pick a product**, pick the product, then the size, and tap **Sell**. Each product has a number on the price sheet: tap **Type** and type it (for example **#12**) to jump to it. If Traced asks you to scan the piece in the customer's hand, the pieces left of that size have labels — scan the one they're holding.

The price sheet is in the menu (**⋮**) › **Price sheet**.

## A return
1. Tap **Return** and scan the piece. No label? Tap **No label? Find the sale**.
2. Choose **Good** or **Damaged**.
3. Choose how you give the money back, then tap **Confirm refund**.
4. Give the money back only when the screen says **RETURNED**.

A good piece can be sold again. Put a damaged piece aside: it won't be sold again and goes back to the warehouse.

## An exchange
1. Tap **Exchange** and scan the piece coming back. Choose **Good** or **Damaged**.
2. Scan the piece the customer takes instead. If it has a label, scan it — don't pick it from the list.
3. Check who pays the difference, then tap **Confirm exchange**.

## Two labels in view
The camera saw two labels at once and sold nothing. Move closer, so only one label is in the picture, and scan again. After each sale, move the camera off the label — or tap **Scan next** — before the next one.

Camera blocked? Allow the camera in your browser's settings, or tap **Type** and type the code.

## Weak signal
If the screen says **Checking…**, wait. Traced keeps trying, and a sale is never recorded twice — don't scan the piece again. There is no offline mode. If the page closed while it was checking, look in **Earlier today** before you scan again.

## The link doesn't work
If it says **This sign-in link can't be used**, ask the owner for a new link. There is no PIN reset: if you lose access to Traced, the owner sends you a new link.

## Not for sellers
Closing the day and ending the bazar are for the owner or a manager.
