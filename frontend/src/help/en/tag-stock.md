Pieces received with Quick count have no label yet. Go to Inventory › Untagged, print labels for the products you want, stick them on, then scan each label so Traced ties it to a piece.

1. Go to **Inventory** › **Untagged**. It lists the pieces in the main warehouse that don't have a label yet.
2. Tick the products you want to label and set how many labels for each (**Select all** takes every product). Press **Print labels**.
3. Stick one label on each piece of the product named on the label.
4. Press **Start scanning** and scan each label you just stuck on. You don't pick a product — the label already knows it.
5. Press **End session** when you are done.

## Labels you didn't use
They stay valid for their product, so you can scan them in a later session. At the end of a session you can **Void unused labels** if they were thrown away. Labels left over from ended sessions appear under **Unused labels** in the same tab, where you can **Reprint** or **Void** them.

A label got torn while tagging? **Print 1 replacement label** in the scan screen asks which product it is for.

## Why tag?
Until it has a label, a piece can't be reprinted, transferred, put on hold or marked damaged, and it can't be put on a shelf. Untagged pieces also get their label when they are packed in Pick & Pack, so you don't have to tag everything at once.

Tagging doesn't change your Shopify stock: the pieces were already added when you received them.
