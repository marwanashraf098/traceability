The gather list adds up everything the orders in Pick & Pack still need, one row per product, so you collect it all from the shelves in one walk. Open it with the **Gather** button on **Pick & Pack**.

## What each column means
- **Item** and **SKU** — the product.
- **Needed** — how many units the orders still need. Pieces already scanned into an order are taken off.
- **Available** — how many pieces of that product Traced has as available.
- **Orders** — the order numbers that need it.

A row in red is short: it says, for example, "Short 2 — need 5, have 3". Collect what you have and check the rest before you start packing.

## How to use it
1. Open **Pick & Pack** and press **Gather**.
2. Press **Print** if you want it on paper.
3. Walk the shelves and collect each item.
4. Go back with **Back to Queue** and pack the orders as usual.

The list isn't saved. It's built again every time you open it or press **Refresh**, from the same orders Pick & Pack shows. Nothing is reserved by opening it.

## Good to know
- If your store uses places, the list shows where each item is and is sorted in walking order.
- For one print batch only: when you print waybills with **Also print a pick list**, press **Open pick list** to get the list for that batch's orders.
