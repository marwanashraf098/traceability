While a bazar is live, press **More** › **Add more pieces** on its page, list the pieces you are adding, then press **Send these to the bazar**. Nothing moves until you send.

## Open the list
- On the computer: the bazar's page › **More** › **Add more pieces**.
- On the phone: the bazar menu › **Add more pieces**. It opens the same screen, which has no camera scanning yet.
- It's there only for owners and managers, and only while the bazar is live: after **Start event** and before you end it.

The screen is titled **Batch N · pieces to add**. Each time you add pieces is one batch, sent on the bazar's same transfer.

## List the pieces
1. Scan each piece you are adding. Press ✕ on a scan to take that piece off the list.
2. For pieces without labels, use **Send by count**: pick the size and how many, then press **Add N**. It takes only pieces without labels at the main warehouse. If some of that size have labels, it adds the ones without and tells you to scan the rest.
3. To take several of a size off the list, use **Remove** on its line.

**The list moves nothing.** The pieces stay at the main warehouse, your Shopify stock doesn't change, and they can still sell online until you send.

You can leave and come back. While a list is open, the bazar's page says **You're adding N pieces to this bazar — not sent yet** with **Continue**, and the menu item reads **Continue adding pieces**. There is one list at a time.

## Send them
Press **Send these N to the bazar**. All the listed pieces go together, or none do:
- **Remove from online stock**: these pieces come off your Shopify stock when you send them.
- **Keep showing online**: your Shopify stock stays as it is. Each piece sold at the bazar comes off afterwards, as for the rest of the bazar.
- If a listed piece changed since you listed it (it sold online, or it's on another transfer), nothing is sent and the message names it. Take it off the list and send again.

Once sent, the pieces are part of the bazar like the rest. When the bazar has had more than one send, its page shows **Sent in N batches**, with the time and the pieces of each.

## Discard the list
Press **Discard this list**, then press it again to confirm. Nothing had moved, so nothing changes.

## Prices for new sizes
A size you add is priced like the rest of the bazar. A size with no price can't be sold. When there are such sizes, the bazar's page says **N sizes have no price** — press **Set prices**. See [Set bazar prices](/help/bazar-prices).

## Before you end the bazar
**End event** is refused while a list is open: send it or discard it first. The leftovers of every batch come back together when the bazar ends — see [End a bazar and bring the leftovers back](/help/bazar-end-event).

## A Keep bazar after Keep became unavailable
If the bazar is on **Keep showing online** and Keep is no longer available for your store, **Send** is refused. The online stock choice can't be changed after the first send, so this bazar can't take more pieces. Discard the list.
