Traced tracks orders placed from the day you connected Shopify. Older orders are not imported, even if they change in Shopify later, so you finish them the way you did before Traced.

## What this means

- The day you first connect your store is fixed. Reconnecting later doesn't move it.
- Orders placed before it don't appear in **Orders** or **Pick & Pack**. Pack and ship them as you used to.
- Bosta deliveries for those orders are left out too — Traced doesn't link them or follow them.
- Their pieces were never received in Traced, so nothing in Traced's stock changes when they ship.

## Returns of older orders

- When one of these parcels comes back, Traced has no record of it, so **Scan returns** can't match it to an order.
- Your returns portal looks up orders that Traced holds, so a customer with an older order may not find it there. Handle that return with the customer directly.
- If the item is good and you put it back on your shelf, add it in **Receiving** like new stock — that adds it to Traced Main Warehouse in Shopify. Don't also restock it in Shopify, or it will be counted twice.

## Orders placed after you connected

Every order placed after you connected comes into Traced. To understand the labels you see on those orders, see [Tracked and not tracked orders](/help/tracked-vs-not-tracked).
