The **Orders** page sorts your orders into tabs by where they are — **To ship**, **In transit**, **Delivered**, **Returned** and **Cancelled** — plus **All** and **Needs attention**. Each order is in only one of the shipping tabs.

## The tabs
- **All** — every order.
- **Needs attention** — orders with an open alert. The chips under it narrow the list by what you need to do: **Can't ship yet**, **No courier update**, **Delivery failed**, **Cancelled but shipped**, **Return to handle** and **Other**.
- **To ship** — not cancelled and not handed over yet: no waybill, or a waybill the courier hasn't moved. An order Shopify shows as fulfilled with no waybill in Traced was shipped outside Traced, so it isn't here.
- **In transit** — with the courier, including delivery problems and parcels on their way back.
- **Delivered** — delivered, or a self-pickup order the customer collected.
- **Returned** — the courier brought it back.
- **Cancelled** — cancelled orders, whatever happened to their shipment.

An order whose shipment was lost or terminated shows under **All** only.

## Filters
Press **Filters** to narrow any tab:
- **Traced** — Traced or Not traced (Bosta orders only — see [Tracked and not tracked orders](/help/tracked-vs-not-tracked)).
- **Returns** — Pending inspection (a returned piece scanned in, not yet restocked or marked damaged) or Inspected.
- **Courier** — Bosta or Other couriers.
- **Payment** — Cash on delivery or Prepaid.
- **Order date** — Last 7, 30 or 90 days, or All time. The list starts at the last 30 days. The date isn't applied while you search or on Needs attention.

## Search
Search by order number, customer name or phone, or use the **Tracking number** box. Your tab, filters and search stay in the page address, so a link you share opens the same view.

If an order is missing, press **Sync from Shopify** to bring in the latest orders.
