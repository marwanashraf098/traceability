In Settings › Places, Print stickers lets you choose a size for each kind of place: 50×25 mm for a label printer, or A6 or A4 for a paper printer. Everything prints in one PDF.

## Where to print from
In **Settings** › **Places**:
- **Print all stickers** for the whole location.
- **Print zone stickers** for one zone.
- **Print sticker** on a selected shelf or box, or **Print stickers** on a selected rack.
- **Create and print stickers** when you create new places.

## Choose the sizes
The **Print stickers** window lists each kind with how many there are: **Zones**, **Racks**, **Shelves** and **White boxes**.
1. Tick the kinds you want.
2. Pick a size for each: **50×25 mm** (label printer), **A6** or **A4** (paper printer).
3. For A6, choose **A6 labels** (one per page) or **4 per A4 sheet**.
4. Press **Print**.

Each kind prints in its own size, all in one PDF. Traced remembers the sizes you chose for next time. Until you choose, shelves start at 50×25 mm, boxes and racks at A6, and zones at A4. Printing a whole location or zone starts with only shelves and boxes ticked.

## Good to know
- A sticker's barcode never changes. Renaming a place or moving a box keeps its sticker valid, so you only reprint if a sticker is damaged or you want the new name on it.
- Only shelves and boxes hold pieces. Zone and rack stickers help people find their way, and a zone sticker is scanned to put a box on that zone's floor.
- Stick shelf stickers on the shelf edge, where they are easy to scan.
