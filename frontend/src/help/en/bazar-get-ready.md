On the bazar's page, scan out the pieces you are taking, set your prices, press **Mark as sent** when the stock leaves the warehouse, then press **Start event**.

## 1. Scan pieces out
1. Press **Scan pieces out**.
2. Scan each piece you are taking. Press ✕ on a recent scan to take that piece off the send.
3. For pieces without labels, use **Send by count** on the same screen — see [Send pieces by count](/help/bazar-send-by-count).
4. Press **Done — back to the event**.

You can come back with **Scan more pieces out** until you mark the stock as sent. The pieces travel on one transfer, which you also find in **Transfers**, tagged Bazar. The leftovers come back on the same transfer when the bazar ends.

## 2. Set prices
Use the **Bazar prices** card — see [Set bazar prices](/help/bazar-prices).

## 3. Mark as sent
When the stock leaves the warehouse, press **Mark N pieces as sent** and confirm with **Mark as sent**. After that:
- you can't scan more pieces out for this bazar;
- the **Online stock** choice can't be changed;
- with **Remove from online stock**, the pieces come off your Shopify stock now. With **Keep showing online**, your Shopify stock doesn't change.

## 4. Start event
Press **Start event**. It works once the stock is sent and no other bazar is live at the same location.

Traced doesn't check the dates or the prices when you start. A product without a price can't be sold until you give it one, so check the prices card first.

## Sellers
**Invite sellers** takes you to the Sellers card — see [Invite sellers](/help/bazar-invite-sellers).

## Delete a bazar
**Delete bazar** is there only while nothing has been scanned out. It also cancels the bazar's transfer.
