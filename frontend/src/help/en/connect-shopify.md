Go to **Settings › Connections**, paste your store's address, tap **Find my store**, then **Connect this store** and approve Traced in Shopify. Only the account owner can connect a store.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings › Connections**. (New accounts see the same step during setup.)
2. Paste your store's `.myshopify.com` address, or copy the whole address bar from your Shopify admin and paste that. Not sure where to find it? Tap **Where do I find this?**
3. Tap **Find my store**. Traced shows the store it found — check it's yours.
4. Tap **Connect this store**. Shopify opens and asks you to approve Traced. Approve, and you come straight back to Traced.

## What Traced does after connecting

- It imports your products, and the orders placed from the day you connect. Older orders are not brought in — see [Orders placed before you connected](/help/orders-before-connect).
- It adds a location called **Traced Main Warehouse** to your Shopify. This is the only place Traced ever changes stock, and it changes it only through what you do in Traced: receiving, shipping, returns, stock takes and adjustments.
- It never edits your products, prices or customers.
- It asks for your main warehouse address, which it also puts on that Shopify location — Shopify needs it to show your products as in stock.

Before you start receiving, read [Why you set old Shopify locations to 0](/help/old-location-zero).

## One store per account

A Traced account is linked to one Shopify store. A store already linked to another Traced account can't be connected here. If you disconnect, you can only reconnect that same store.

## If something goes wrong

- **Reconnect your store** means Shopify access expired. Tap **Reconnect** and syncing picks up where it left off.
- If you see **The official Traced app is live**, tap **Upgrade to the official app**. Your data and history stay as they are.
