On a warehouse tablet in station mode, the worker who's finishing taps **Switch / Lock** in their menu. The tablet goes back to **Who's working?**, and the next worker taps their name and enters their PIN.

## Hand over the tablet

1. Open your menu at the top of the screen and tap **Switch / Lock**.
2. The tablet shows **Who's working?**. Nothing of yours stays open.
3. The next worker taps their name, enters their 4-digit PIN and starts on their home screen.

Leaving the tablet for a while? Tap **Switch / Lock** too — nobody can use it until someone enters their PIN.

Every scan and every action is recorded under the worker who was signed in, so each person should use their own name and PIN.

## If the PIN doesn't work

- **Incorrect PIN. Try again.** Check the digits and type it again. Picked the wrong name? Tap **← Switch worker**.
- After five wrong PINs in a row, that worker is locked for 15 minutes and the screen shows **PIN locked** with the time left. Tap **Back to roster**; other workers can still sign in.
- A worker's name isn't on the list? They must be an active user with a PIN — ask an owner or manager to check **Settings › Users**.

The PIN is set when the worker is added. It can't be changed from the Users page.

## Paired phones

A phone paired as a scanner belongs to the worker who paired it. Switching workers ends that pairing, so the next worker pairs their own phone from a scanning screen with **Use phone**.
