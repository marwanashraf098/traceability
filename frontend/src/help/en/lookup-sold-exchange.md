Find an available piece in Lookup to mark it **Sold** when you sold it outside Shopify, or to send it as the replacement in an **Exchange**. Only owners and managers can do either.

## Sold

Use this for a sale in person, on Instagram or at a bazar.

1. Scan or search the piece. It must be available.
2. Tap **Sold**, add a note if you like, and tap **Mark sold**.
3. The piece shows **Sold (Off-book)**, and Traced takes 1 off "Traced Main" in Shopify.

Marked it by mistake? Open the piece and tap **Not sold**, then **Yes, not sold**. It goes back to available, and if the sale reached Shopify, Shopify goes back up by 1.

## Exchange

**Exchange** appears only on stores that ship with their own couriers: **I ship with other couriers** is on in Settings › Couriers and no Bosta account is connected. The piece must be available, labelled and at your main warehouse.

1. Open the piece and tap **Exchange**.
2. Find the customer's original delivered order by number, name or phone.
3. Choose the item that's coming back.
4. Optionally choose the courier and enter the **Tracking number (going out)** — you can add it later.
5. Tap **Create exchange**.

Without a tracking number, the piece is reserved for the exchange and stays on the shelf. Once you enter the tracking number, the piece is handed to the courier and Traced takes 1 off "Traced Main" in Shopify. The item coming back shows in returns, waiting to be scanned in **Scan returns**.

Until the piece leaves, you can open the exchange and tap **Cancel exchange**: the piece goes back to available and nothing changes in Shopify.

In some cases Traced doesn't change Shopify stock, for example before Shopify is counting at your main warehouse; the item's history shows why.
