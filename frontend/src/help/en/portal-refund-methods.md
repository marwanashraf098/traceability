Under **Refund methods** in **Settings › Returns portal**, tick the ways you're willing to refund. Customers asking for a refund pick one and give their details. Traced never sends money — you refund them yourself.

## The methods

- **Bank transfer** — the customer gives the account holder, bank, and IBAN or account number.
- **InstaPay** — an InstaPay address or phone number.
- **Mobile wallet** — Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, e& cash or WE Pay, and the wallet number.
- **Cash** — no details asked, for example when the courier or your shop pays them back.

Tick the ones you use and tap **Save changes**. With none ticked, customers aren't asked how they want their refund.

For an exchange, customers who agree to a refund if their new size sells out are asked the same question, so you can refund them if you have to.

## Where you see the details

Open the request in **Returns & exchanges**. Under **Customer asked for**, tap **Show details** to see what the customer entered, with a copy button for each field.

- Only owners and managers can see them.
- They're deleted 30 days after the request ends.

## Recording the refund

Once the items are back and checked, the request shows **Record the refund**. Traced suggests an amount and fills in the method the customer chose — change it if you paid another way. Tap **Save refund**, then **Mark as refunded** when you're done. See [Approve or reject requests](/help/portal-requests).
