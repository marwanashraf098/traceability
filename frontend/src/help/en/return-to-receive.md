A **Return To Receive** alert means a returned item came back sellable but Traced couldn't put it in stock, because it never tracked that order. Add the item in your next Receiving session from the alert, then resolve the alert.

## Where it comes from

In **Scan returns**, someone marked an item from an untracked order **Arrived · sellable**, or marked the whole parcel received. A sellable untracked item from a customer's return request raises the same kind of alert, called **Returned Item To Receive**. Stock and Shopify haven't changed yet.

## What to do

1. Open **Alerts** and find the alert. It shows the order, the item and who marked it received.
2. Tap **Go →**. Receiving opens with a banner: "Receiving a return of order …".
3. Open a Receiving session at your main warehouse, or start one. The banner can't add the item to a **High control · Label & verify** session.
4. Tap **Add 1 to this session**. The item appears under "Returned items in this session", linked to the return.
5. Finish the session as usual. The item goes into stock at your main warehouse, and Traced adds it to "Traced Main" in Shopify.
6. Back in **Alerts**, tap **Resolve**.

If the item won't go back into stock, just resolve the alert.

## Shopify already restocked it?

If Shopify already restocked this item for the order, the banner tells you. Traced still adds the piece to its own stock but doesn't add it to Shopify again, so it isn't counted twice. For the same reason, don't restock these items in Shopify yourself.

In some cases Traced doesn't change Shopify stock, for example before Shopify is counting at your main warehouse; the item's history shows why.
