When exchanges are on, customers can ask in your returns portal for another size or colour of the same product. Approving books one Bosta exchange trip: the courier brings the new item and collects the old one in the same visit.

## Turn it on

In **Settings › Returns portal**, in this order:

1. Connect Bosta (Settings › Connections).
2. Under **Returns go back to**, choose the Bosta pickup location returns should come back to.
3. Turn on **Book Bosta pickups when I approve**, and tap **Save changes**.
4. Turn on **Allow exchanges** and save.

Each switch tells you what's missing if you can't turn it on yet.

## What the customer does

They pick one item, choose a new size or colour that's in stock, and can tick that they're happy to get a refund instead if it sells out before you approve. One item per exchange request.

## What you do

1. Open the request in **Returns & exchanges** and tap **Approve exchange**. Traced checks the new size is still in stock.
2. Traced books the Bosta exchange trip and adds the replacement to **Pick & Pack**. Pack it and hand it to Bosta in **Pickups** like any order.
3. When the old item comes back, scan it in **Scan returns** and grade it. The request then shows **Exchanged**.

If the new size sold out after the customer asked, you can **Switch to refund and approve** (only when the customer agreed to a refund) or reject the request.

## Shopify stock

When the replacement leaves your warehouse, Traced takes 1 off "Traced Main" in Shopify. When the old item is restocked, it adds 1 back.

## Good to know

Exchanges approved before you turned on **Allow exchanges** aren't booked automatically — use **Book now** in the request. If Bosta doesn't confirm a booking, the request tells you to check in Bosta before you retry.
