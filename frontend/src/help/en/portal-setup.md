Your returns portal is a page where customers start a return or exchange themselves. Choose its address in **Settings › Returns portal**, turn on **Customers can start returns**, save, and add the link to your Shopify store.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings › Returns portal**.
2. Under **Portal link**, type the address you want after returns.tracedtech.com/ — 3 to 40 lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, for example your store's name.
3. Turn on **Customers can start returns**.
4. Set the **Return window**: how many days after delivery customers can ask (1 to 90).
5. Choose **How requests are approved**: **I review each request**, or **Approve automatically**.
6. Tap **Save changes**. The page header shows **Live**.
7. Tap **Copy link** and add it to your store's footer, or to a "Returns" page, in Shopify.
8. Tap **Open portal** to try it the way a customer sees it.

## The rest of the page

- **Eligibility** — the return window, products that can't be returned, photos, and returns for orders shipped with other couriers. See [Who can request a return](/help/portal-who-can-return).
- **Approval & pickup** — automatic approval, where Bosta brings returns back to, and booking Bosta pickups when you approve.
- **Exchanges** — let customers swap for another size or colour. See [Exchanges with Bosta, in one trip](/help/exchanges-bosta).
- **Refund methods** — see [Choose refund methods](/help/portal-refund-methods).
- **Branding & policy** — see [Add your logo and font](/help/portal-branding).

**Your policy in plain words** at the top sums up your current settings as customers will experience them. It updates as you change things.

## Turning it off

Turn off **Customers can start returns** and save. Your link then shows "This returns page isn't available", and your settings are kept for when you turn it back on.

Owners and managers can change these settings; workers don't see this tab.
