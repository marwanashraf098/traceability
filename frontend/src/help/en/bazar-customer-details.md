When a customer asks what you hold about them, or asks you to delete it, go to **Bazar** › **Customer details**, find them, then press **Export CSV** or **Delete details**.

## Find the customer
1. Type their phone (010…, +20 10…, or with Arabic digits) or at least 2 letters of their name, then press **Search**.
2. Tick the customer. If the same person shows up more than once — with and without a phone — tick each one.

Each customer shows their sales, the bazars they bought at, the pieces they kept, the total and their last visit.

## Export their details
Press **Export CSV**. You get every one of their bazar sales — undone ones included — with the bazar, day, time, sale number, product, price, payment method, status and their name and phone. Owners and managers can export, and every export is logged.

## Delete their details
Only the owner can delete. Press **Delete details**. Traced first shows how many sales and bazars it affects, then press **Delete details** again to confirm.
- Their name and phone are removed from those sales.
- The sales, amounts and items stay, so your reports and money don't change.
- This can't be undone.

This covers bazar sales only. Privacy requests that come through Shopify don't include bazar sales, so this page is where you handle them.
