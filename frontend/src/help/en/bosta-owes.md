Cash on delivery that Bosta collected but hasn't paid out yet is shown as **Delivered, awaiting payout** on the **Summary** page in **Analytics**. The **Bosta & payouts** page lets you check each payout and lists deliveries that are late to be paid.

## On the Summary page

**Cash position: where your money is** follows your money in four steps:

1. **Not yet fulfilled**: orders that haven't left yet, at face value.
2. **In transit with Bosta**: the cash on delivery of parcels on the way, expected at each governorate's delivery success rate.
3. **Delivered, awaiting payout**: collected by Bosta, not yet sent to you. This is what Bosta owes you now. A line under it can add deliveries Bosta hasn't settled yet, as an estimate.
4. **In your bank**: what Bosta already paid, with the date of the last transfer.

**Expected cash in** splits what's coming into the next 7 days, 8–14 days and 15–30 days. It's based on Bosta's weekly payout day and how long payouts usually take for you. Until there are enough payouts, the card says it can't tell your payout day yet.

## On the Bosta & payouts page

1. **Payout check** lists each Bosta payout with the Traced orders matched to it. **Traced amount** is what Bosta deposited for the shipments Traced tracks; **Bosta batch total** is the whole payout. A payout can show **Paid** or **Scheduled**, and can include money from shipments Traced doesn't track.
2. **Shipments stuck for more than 7 days** lists cash at risk: **Never picked up**, **Stuck with Bosta**, and **Delivered, not paid**. Send these tracking numbers to Bosta and ask what happened.

## Things to know

- All of this needs Bosta connected. Orders shipped with another courier aren't in these figures.
- Some fees can be Bosta quotes rather than settled amounts; they're marked **estimated**.
- Analytics is for the account owner only.
