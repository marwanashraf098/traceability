**Log out** in your menu signs out only the device you're on. To sign your account out everywhere at once, go to **Settings › Users** and use **Log out of all devices**.

## Log out of this device

Open your menu at the top of the screen and tap **Log out**. Only this browser or tablet is signed out. Your other devices, and a warehouse tablet running on your account, keep working.

On a warehouse tablet in station mode, a worker's menu shows **Switch / Lock** instead. It hands the tablet to the next worker without signing the tablet out — see [Switch workers with a PIN](/help/pin-switch).

## Log out of all devices

Use this when a phone or laptop is lost, or you signed in on a computer that isn't yours.

1. Go to **Settings › Users**.
2. Under **Log out of all devices**, tap **Log out of all devices**.
3. Tap **Yes, log out everywhere**.

Every session of your account ends: other browsers, phones, and any warehouse tablet that is signed in as you. This device goes back to the sign-in page. Sign in again wherever you still need Traced, including a warehouse tablet that ran on your account — see [Set up the warehouse tablet](/help/tablet-setup).

This option is on the Settings page, so owners and managers can use it for their own account. It signs out only your account, not anyone else's.

## Someone else's access

To stop a person from signing in — for example, someone who left — an owner or manager deactivates them in **Settings › Users**. Their history stays, and they can be reactivated later.

Signing out also ends a phone paired as a scanner, so pair it again next time.
