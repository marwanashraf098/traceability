Traced sends its emails from no-reply@tracedtech.com. If they land in junk or spam, mark one as "Not spam" and add that address to your contacts, so the next ones reach your inbox.

## Which emails Traced sends

- **Welcome to Traced** — once, when you create your account.
- **Reset your password** — the code you asked for on the forgot-password page. It goes only to the person who asked.
- Urgent alert emails — when a critical or high alert opens (for example, stock that didn't reach Shopify). Traced checks every few minutes and emails each alert once.
- **Daily exceptions summary** — every morning at 8:00 (Cairo time), when there are open alerts.
- **A customer data request is ready** — when a Shopify customer asks for their data. Only the owner gets this one.

Alert emails go to every active owner and manager who has an email address. Workers don't get emails.

## Get them into your inbox

1. Search your junk or spam folder for "Traced".
2. Open one and mark it **Not spam** (Gmail: **Report not spam**; Outlook: **Not junk**).
3. Add no-reply@tracedtech.com to your contacts or safe senders.
4. In Gmail, also check the **Promotions** and **Updates** tabs.
5. On a work email, ask whoever runs it to allow mail from tracedtech.com.

## Still not arriving?

- Check the email address shown for you in **Settings › Users** — alerts go to that address.
- You don't need the email to act: every alert is also on the **Alerts** page in Traced.
- Message us on WhatsApp at +20 103 737 1204 and we'll check what was sent.
