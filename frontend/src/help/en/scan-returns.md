Open **Scan returns**, start a return session, scan the parcel's waybill and then each item's Traced label. Decide restock or damaged for every item, then close the session.

## Steps

1. Go to **Scan returns** and tap **Open return session**. Your store has one open session at a time — if one is already open, tap **Resume session** instead.
2. Scan the waybill on the returned parcel. Traced opens a card for that parcel showing the order, the customer and the items it expects back. Parcels Bosta has already brought back to you are listed as waiting to be scanned.
3. Open the parcel and scan the Traced label on each item. Each scanned item appears with **Restock** and **Damage** buttons. A label is missing? Use **Reprint label** on the item.
4. Choose **Restock** or **Damage** for every scanned item. See [Grade returns: restock or damaged](/help/grade-returns).
5. Tap **Close session**. You can't close while any scanned item still needs a decision.

## Things to know

- An item marked **UNEXPECTED** came back without Traced expecting it (for example, it isn't on the scanned parcel's order). Check it before you decide.
- If the order was shipped before Traced tracked its items, there are no labels to scan. You mark each item instead — see [A return from an order Traced never tracked](/help/untracked-returns).
- You can scan with a paired phone as well as a scanner — see [Scan with your phone](/help/phone-scanner).
- A session left open with nothing waiting for a decision closes by itself after about 12 hours without activity.

## Who can do what

Owners, managers and workers can open a session, scan, restock, mark damaged and close. Only owners and managers can see the list of past sessions, abandon a session, or mark an item **Not the real piece**.
