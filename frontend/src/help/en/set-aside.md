In waybill scan mode, press **Set aside this order** when you can't finish an order now — a piece is missing or damaged, or the waybill can't be read. The pieces you already scanned go back to stock, and the order waits for a manager to sort it out.

1. With the order open in your pack session, press **Set aside this order**.
2. Choose a **Reason**: **Piece missing on the shelf**, **Damaged piece**, **Waybill damaged or unreadable** or **Other**.
3. Press **Set aside**. To go back instead, press **Keep packing**.
4. Put any pieces you scanned back on the shelf, and put the waybill to one side for a manager.
5. Scan the next waybill and carry on.

You can't end the session while an order is open: finish it or set it aside first.

## What happens next
- The order shows as **Set aside** with its reason in **Printed but not packed** on the Pick & Pack page.
- An alert, **Set Aside While Packing**, appears in **Alerts**, and the order shows under **Needs attention** on **Orders**.
- The order stays in Pick & Pack. Once the problem is fixed, scan its waybill again and pack it as usual.
- The alert goes away when the order is packed or cancelled. A manager can also press **Resolve** on it.

## Set aside by Traced
If the waybill is deleted in Bosta while you're packing the order, Traced sets the order aside by itself with the reason **Waybill deleted in Bosta**. Give the waybill to a manager — the order needs a new waybill.
