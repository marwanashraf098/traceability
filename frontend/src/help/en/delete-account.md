There is no delete button in Traced. To close your account, the account owner asks us on WhatsApp ([wa.me/201037371204](https://wa.me/201037371204)) or by email at **tracedtechnology@gmail.com**.

## How to ask

1. Send the request as the account owner.
2. Include your store name and the email you sign in to Traced with.
3. Say when you want it closed, for example after your last returns have come back.

## What happens to your data

Traced's privacy policy explains this in its **Retention** section. In short:

- Account and operational data (your products, pieces, orders and their history) is kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, accounting and security obligations. After that it is deleted or anonymised.
- Your customers' personal details (name, phone, address) are erased when Shopify sends a valid redaction request for them or for your store.
- When customer details are erased, the per-piece custody history stays, because it holds no customer personal data.
- Refund account details from the returns portal are deleted 30 days after the request ends, and customer photos 90 days after it ends.

## Your Shopify store

If you uninstall the Traced app in Shopify, Traced stops receiving your orders and stops updating stock.

Questions about your data can go to the same email address.
