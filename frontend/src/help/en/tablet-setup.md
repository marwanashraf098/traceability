Sign in on the tablet as an owner or manager, open **Settings › Users** and tap **Enter station mode**. The tablet becomes a shared station: workers pick their name and type their PIN instead of using an email and password.

## Before you start

Add each worker in **Settings › Users** with **Add user**, choose the **Worker** role and give them a 4-digit PIN. Only active users with a PIN appear on the station.

## Turn on station mode

1. On the tablet, sign in to Traced with an owner or manager account.
2. Go to **Settings › Users**. Under **Station mode**, tap **Enter station mode**.
3. The tablet shows **Who's working?** with your workers' names.
4. A worker taps their name and enters their PIN. They land on their home screen with their tasks.

Station mode belongs to this tablet only and stays on after a reload or restart. Each time the tablet is reloaded or restarted, it goes back to **Who's working?** and the worker enters their PIN again.

## Scanners

- Connect a USB or Bluetooth scanner to the tablet and test it — see [Test your barcode scanner](/help/test-scanner).
- Or pair a phone as the tablet's scanner from any scanning screen — see [Use your phone as a scanner](/help/phone-scanner).

## Turn station mode off

1. On the **Who's working?** screen, tap **Exit station mode**.
2. Sign in with an owner or manager email and password. A worker can't exit station mode.

## Good to know

- Five wrong PINs in a row lock that worker for 15 minutes. Other workers can still sign in.
- Hand the tablet over with **Switch / Lock** — see [Switch workers with a PIN](/help/pin-switch).
