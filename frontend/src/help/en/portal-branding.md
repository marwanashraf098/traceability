In **Settings › Returns portal › Branding & policy** you can add your logo, pick a font and a brand colour, and write the return policy customers read. A preview in English and Arabic updates as you edit.

## Logo

1. Drag your logo onto the **Logo** box, or choose a file. Use a PNG, JPG or WebP up to 2 MB — a transparent PNG looks best.
2. The logo saves as soon as it uploads; your returns page shows it right away.
3. To change it, use **Replace** or **Remove**.

Prefer a file you already keep in Shopify? Use **Use a Shopify Files link instead** and paste its link (it starts with https://cdn.shopify.com/). An uploaded logo is used first; the link is used only when no logo is uploaded.

## Font

Under **Font**, pick one of five fonts: Cairo (the default), Tajawal, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Almarai or Readex Pro. The font is used on your returns page in both English and Arabic.

## Brand colour

**Brand colour** is used for buttons and links on your returns page. Enter a hex colour such as #1A2B3C or pick one. If the colour is light, button text switches to dark so it stays readable. If a colour is hard to read with both white and dark text, the portal uses the default blue instead.

## Return policy

Paste your return policy under **Return policy** (up to 2,000 characters). Customers can open it before sending a request. If your text mentions a number of days that differs from your return window, Traced warns you — customers see both, so make them match.

## Saving

Font, colour and policy save with **Save changes**. The logo saves on its own when you upload or remove it.
