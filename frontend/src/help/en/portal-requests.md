Customer requests from your returns portal appear on the **Returns & exchanges** page (**Requests** in the menu). Open one to see the items, reasons and photos, then tap **Approve** or **Reject**.

## Find requests that need you

The **To approve** tile and the **To do** tab show the requests waiting for a decision. You can search by order, customer, waybill or the request's reference (RR-…).

## Decide

1. Click a request to open it. You see the customer, the order and its delivery date, the items with their reasons, the customer's note and any photos.
2. Tap **Approve**, or tap **Reject**, type a reason and tap **Reject request**. The customer sees the reason.
3. If you turned on **Book Bosta pickups when I approve**, approving books a Bosta courier to collect the items from the customer's area. You can **Change area** first if needed. Otherwise, book the pickup in Bosta yourself.

If automatic approval is on, requests arrive already approved.

## After approval

1. When the parcel comes back, scan it in **Scan returns** and grade each item. See [Scan returned parcels](/help/scan-returns).
2. The request shows **Refund to record**. Open it, check the suggested amount, and use **Record the refund** → **Save refund**.
3. Tap **Mark as refunded**. The request is done.

Traced only records the refund — you send the money yourself.

## When not everything comes back

- **The rest isn't coming** — stop waiting for the items that haven't arrived.
- **Close without refund…** or **Close request…** — end the request without a refund, with a reason.

## If a Bosta booking fails

The request says what went wrong. Use **Retry** when Bosta refused it. If Traced couldn't confirm whether the pickup was booked, check in Bosta first, then choose **It wasn't booked — retry** or **It was booked — enter tracking number**.
