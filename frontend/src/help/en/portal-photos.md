Customers can always add up to 3 photos of each item they return. Turn on **Require photos** in **Settings › Returns portal** to make at least one photo per item a must — it helps you spot damage before the courier collects it.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Settings › Returns portal**, section **Eligibility**.
2. Turn on **Require photos**.
3. Tap **Save changes**.

## What the customer sees

Each item they choose has a **Photos** box. With the switch on it says **Required**, and they can't continue until every item has at least one photo. With it off, photos are optional. For several units of the same item, one set of photos covers all of them.

They can take photos or choose them from their phone: JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 8 MB each, at most 3 per item.

## Where you see them

Open the request in **Returns & exchanges**. The photos show next to each item; tap one to view it larger and move between them.

- Only owners and managers can see photos.
- Photos are removed 90 days after the request ends.
