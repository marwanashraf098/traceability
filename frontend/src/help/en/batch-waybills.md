Press **Print waybills** on **Pick & Pack** to print the Bosta waybills of the orders ready to pack in one go. Traced saves each print as a numbered batch, so you can see later which waybills were printed but not packed.

1. Open **Pick & Pack** and press **Print waybills**.
2. Under **Which orders**, choose **New only** (orders with no printed waybill yet) or **Everything ready to pack** (this also reprints the ones already printed).
3. Choose the **Paper size** (A4 or A6 thermal) and the **Order**: oldest or newest order first.
4. Tick **Also print a pick list** to get every item of these orders grouped by product, so the picker walks the shelves once.
5. Press the print button — it shows the count, for example **Print 12 waybills**. You can close the window while Traced prepares the waybills — the print keeps going. Open **Print waybills** again to see it, then press **Open waybills** or **Download PDF**.

## If some waybills didn't print
Traced lists every waybill it skipped and why — for example deleted in Bosta, refused by Bosta, or Bosta busy (print again in a few minutes). The PDF is deleted 24 hours after the print; reprint the batch to get it again.

## In waybill scan mode
The Pick & Pack page also shows:
- **Print batches today**, with **Reprint** for each batch. A cancelled order's waybill is not reprinted.
- **Printed but not packed** — every printed waybill whose order isn't packed yet, including orders that were set aside or [cancelled after the print](/help/cancelled-after-print).

## Good to know
- Only Bosta waybills print here. An order that ships with another courier has no waybill to print in Traced.
- On stores that print piece labels while packing, the button is called **Print** and can print the piece labels too, in the same order as the waybill stack.
