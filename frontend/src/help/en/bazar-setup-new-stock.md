Use **Set up from new stock** for stock that isn't in Traced yet. Traced adds the pieces to Traced Main in your Shopify, prints labels if you want them, and gives you a list to lower at the Shopify location you took them from. Pieces already in Traced? Plan a bazar and send them from stock instead.

## Where to start
- **During setup:** **What will you use Traced for?** asks once. **Bazars & pop-up events** skips the warehouse address and courier steps and opens this setup after Shopify. **Both** sets up orders and shipping first, then offers **Set up from new stock** when setup is done.
- **Later:** **Bazar** › **Set up from new stock**. Only the owner can use it, with Shopify connected.

## The steps
1. **Your bazar**: name, where, the days, how customers pay, who's selling and the online stock choice — see [Online stock: Remove or Keep](/help/bazar-online-stock).
2. **Pick products**: tick a product, then how many of each size. Each size shows how many are in your other Shopify locations. If some are already at Traced Main, Traced warns you — if it's the same stock, don't add it again.
3. **Barcodes**: labels or no barcodes (below). Press **Add N pieces to Traced Main**.
4. **Lower these at the location you took them from** (below).
5. **Prices**: set your bazar prices — see [Set bazar prices](/help/bazar-prices).
6. **Ready**: press **Mark N pieces as sent** when they leave, then **Start event**. With **Keep showing online**, marking them sent doesn't change your Shopify stock.

## Labels or no barcodes
- **Print barcode labels**: one label per piece; scanning it sells it. Each piece is tracked through returns and the report. You need a label printer. In Receiving this is **Label now** (Medium control).
- **No barcodes**: the seller taps the product and size on the phone, and the pieces go to the bazar by count. In Receiving this is **Quick count** (Low control).

You can mix: labels on some products, no barcodes on the rest. Labels print in your store's label size.

## Lower these at the location you took them from
Traced added the pieces to Traced Main in your Shopify, but they came from another Shopify location. In Shopify, lower the same amounts at the location you took them from, so nothing counts twice. Traced doesn't change that location for you.

1. Use the list on screen, or **Download CSV**.
2. If you chose labels, press **Print labels**.
3. Lower each amount in Shopify, then press **Done, I lowered them**.

**Done, I lowered them doesn't check Shopify.** It only records that you did it. If you press it without lowering, Shopify counts those pieces twice. You can't add more pieces or set prices until you press it.

## "Finish setting up Traced Main first"
You see this when Traced Main is linked in your Shopify but Shopify isn't counting your stock there yet, and you already have pieces in Traced. Finish that in **Settings** › **Connections**, then come back.
