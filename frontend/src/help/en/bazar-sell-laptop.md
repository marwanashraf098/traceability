On the live bazar's page, press **Start selling**. It opens the same sell screen sellers use on their phones, and a USB barcode scanner works straight away.

## Sell
- **Quick · scan = sold**: every scan is one sale, paid with the bazar's first payment method. Tap another method on the sale to change how it was paid.
- **Cart**: scan several pieces, add a **Discount** if you want, choose how the customer paid, then press **Charge**.

The screen shows **Scanner ready** while the scanner is ready. **Type** lets you type a code by hand. The camera is off on a computer — **Use camera** turns it on.

## Pieces without a barcode
On a wide screen the products show as tiles: search by name, colour or the **#** from the price sheet, tap the product, then the size. If the pieces left of a size all have labels, Traced asks you to scan the piece in the customer's hand.

## Price sheet
**Price sheet** downloads the bazar's A4 price list. Each product has a number on it, so a seller can type **#12** to find it.

## Returns and exchanges
**Return / exchange** opens the return screen. Undo, returns and exchanges work as on a phone — see [The seller's guide](/help/bazar-seller-guide).

## Leaving the sell screen
**Home** takes you to the bazar's phone home page, not back to the event page. From there, **Go to the rest of Traced** takes you back.

## Phones
The bazar page's **Sell from any phone** card has a QR code. Open Traced on the phone and sign in: the live bazar opens first.
