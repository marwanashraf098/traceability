You can book a free call with the Traced team to set things up together. Pick a time on our calendar: [calendly.com/tracedtechnology/traced-setup-call](https://calendly.com/tracedtechnology/traced-setup-call).

## When it helps

- **Connecting Shopify.** When you connect your store, Traced asks how you'd like to set things up. **Book a setup call** is the easiest option: we connect your store together on a quick call, so you don't have to touch Shopify settings yourself.
- **Your first receiving session.** Before you receive stock for the first time, Traced offers **Book a free setup call** if you'd like someone with you for that part.
- **Anything else.** The **Help center** has a **Book a setup call** button under **Still stuck?**.

## Tips before the call

1. Have your Shopify admin open, signed in as the store owner.
2. If you use Bosta, have your Bosta account at hand too.
3. Have your scanner and label printer plugged in, if you have them, so we can test a scan and a label together.

Connecting your store takes about 10–15 minutes. If you'd rather not wait, you can message us on WhatsApp instead; see [Contact support](/help/contact-support).
