Scan or type the piece's barcode in Lookup to see its status, location and place. To find every piece of a product, press Where is it in Inventory.

## One piece
In **Lookup**, scan the piece's barcode, or type a piece, order or tracking number. You see its **Status**, **Location** and **Place** (or **No place yet**), and its full custody history, including every placement. To change its place, press **Move**, then scan a shelf or box sticker or pick a place.

## Every piece of a product
In **Inventory** › **Stock by location**, press **Where is it** next to a product. It lists each place holding its pieces, with how many are available, on hold or damaged, and how many have no place yet.

To see what is on one shelf or box, choose it in the **Place** filter in Inventory. Boxes on that shelf aren't included. Choose **No place yet** to see the pieces still waiting for a shelf.

## What's on a place
Scan a shelf or box sticker in **Lookup**: Traced shows that it's a place sticker and how many pieces are there. In **Settings** › **Places**, tap a shelf to see what's on it.

## While picking
**Pick & Pack** shows the **Place** of each item to pick.

## Still can't find it?
- The shelf doesn't match? Use **Count one place** to scan a shelf or box. It fixes where pieces are and never writes anything off.
- Gone for good? Mark it lost from Lookup ([Lost, damaged, destroyed, void and on hold](/help/piece-statuses)), or run a [stock take](/help/stock-take).

Without places, Lookup still shows the piece's location and history.
