Turn on Use places in this store in Settings › Places, then build each location's zones, racks, shelves and white boxes and print their stickers. The place features appear in the rest of the app once a location has its first shelf or box.

## Turn places on
1. Go to **Settings** › **Places**. Owners and managers can do this.
2. Switch **Use places in this store** on.
3. If you have more than one location, choose the location you are setting up.

## Set up the first zone
A new location shows one form for its first zone. Set the number of **Racks**, **Shelves in each rack** and **White boxes on the floor**, then press **Create and print stickers**.

## Add and change places
- **Add zone** creates the next zone.
- In a zone, **Add rack** adds a rack, **+ Shelf** adds a shelf to a rack, and **+ Box** or **+ Box here** adds a white box on the zone floor or on a shelf.
- Tap a shelf or box to see what's on it, **Rename** it, print its sticker or **Archive** it.
- **Move this box** puts a box on another shelf or on a zone floor. The pieces inside move with it.
- **Mark as Damaged place** marks a zone or place where you keep damaged stock.
- **Archive zone** removes a zone you no longer use.

A code uses Latin letters, digits, "-", "_" or "." (up to 40 characters).

## How places work
Pieces go on a shelf or in a box — never directly on a zone or a rack. A sticker's barcode never changes, so renaming or moving a place keeps its sticker valid.

Places never change your Shopify stock. They only record where each piece sits.

## Turning places off
Places are hidden but nothing is deleted: everything comes back when you turn it on again. Open placing sessions and place counts are closed; placed pieces stay where they are.
