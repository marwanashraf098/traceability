Place on shelves lists the pieces at a location that don't have a shelf or box yet. Press Start placing, scan a shelf or box sticker, then scan each piece you put there.

1. Open **Place on shelves** from the menu. Its number shows how many pieces are waiting.
2. Filter by where the pieces came from: **Receiving**, **Returns**, **Transfers**, **Stock take** or **Other**. The **Suggested place** is where the piece was last, or where most pieces of the same product sit — you can always scan a different place.
3. Press **Start placing** (or **Resume placing** if you have a session open).
4. Scan the sticker on the shelf edge or the box. Pieces you scan next go into that place.
5. Scan each piece as you put it there. To switch place, scan another shelf or box sticker.
6. Press **Finish**.

## Straight after a job
You don't have to start from the full list. After a job, a button opens a list of just its pieces, grouped by place, which you can print in A6 or A4:
- **Place on shelves now** on a finished receiving session.
- **Place restocked pieces on shelves** in Scan returns.
- **Place returned pieces on shelves** when a transfer comes back.

## Good to know
- Everyone on the team can place pieces, including workers.
- A piece needs a label first. Untagged pieces are refused — tag them, then place them ([Tag stock](/help/tag-stock)).
- Pieces go on a shelf or in a box, not on a zone or rack.
- Scanning a piece that already has a place moves it to the new place.
- To move one piece later, use **Move** in Lookup. To move a whole box, use **Move a box**: scan the box, then the shelf it goes on (or a zone sticker for that zone's floor).
- A session with no activity for 12 hours closes by itself. Closing a session never moves a piece.

Every placement is saved in the piece's history.
