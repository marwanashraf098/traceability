If a damaged piece was repaired or marked damaged by mistake, open it in Lookup and press Back to good. It returns to available and can be sold and picked again.

1. Scan the piece in **Lookup**. Its status shows **Damaged**.
2. Press **Back to good**.
3. Choose the reason: **Repaired**, **Graded damaged by mistake** or **Other**. **Other** needs a note.
4. Press **Return to good**.

The piece is now **Available** in good condition, free for any new order. The change is saved in the piece's history.

## What happens in Shopify
- If the piece was marked damaged in the warehouse, Shopify moved that unit from available to damaged at the Traced Main Warehouse. Back to good moves it back to available.
- If the piece was graded damaged when it came back from a customer, it was never added back to Shopify. Back to good adds 1 — unless Shopify already restocked that unit for the order.

## What can't come back
- **Destroyed** and **Void** pieces are final.
- A piece out on a transfer can't be changed until it returns.

Only owners and managers can do this.
