A transfer tracks pieces that leave your main warehouse. Send out and back is for stock that returns (showroom, dry cleaner, repair); Move to another location is for stock that stays there; Bring back returns stock you moved earlier.

First add the other place as a location in **Settings** › **Locations**. Then go to **Transfers** › **New transfer** and choose what you want to do.

## Send out and back
1. Pick the **Destination** and the **Transfer Type** (Showroom, Dry Clean, Repair or Other). You can add an expected return date and a note. Press **Create Transfer**.
2. Scan each piece that leaves. Press **Mark as sent** when they are on their way. If nothing was scanned, you can **Cancel transfer** instead.
3. When the stock comes back, open the transfer and press **Begin Reconcile**. Scan each returning piece as **Good** or **Condemned**.
4. For pieces that didn't come back, use **Classify shortfall** to record them as sold, lost or condemned.
5. When every line balances, press **Close Transfer**.

## Move to another location
For stock that goes somewhere and stays. Press **Start move**, scan out each piece, then press **Close** and **Confirm Close**. There is no scan-back. The pieces can't be picked from your main warehouse until you bring them back.

## Bring back
For stock you moved earlier. Choose the location it comes **From**; it always goes to your main warehouse. Press **Start bringing back**, scan out the pieces at the source, then scan them back when they arrive. This option appears once you have moved stock before.

## Good to know
- Only owners and managers can run transfers.
- A piece needs a label to be transferred. Tag untagged pieces first ([Tag stock](/help/tag-stock)).
- **Reprint Outstanding Labels** prints the labels of the pieces still out.
- With places on, pieces that come back can be put on shelves straight away ([Place pieces on shelves](/help/put-away)).
- Whether Shopify stock goes down depends on the destination: see [Do transfers lower my Shopify stock?](/help/transfers-shopify)
