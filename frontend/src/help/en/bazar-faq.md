Answers to the questions owners ask most about bazars: the venue's internet, selling the same piece online, online stock that didn't change, and sellers who can't close the day.

## The venue's internet is bad
There is no offline mode: every sale needs the internet. **Tip:** bring a hotspot — a second phone with mobile data.

If the signal drops in the middle of a sale, the screen shows **Checking…** and keeps trying while it stays open. A sale is never recorded twice, so the seller shouldn't scan the piece again. If the page was closed while it was checking, look in **Earlier today** before scanning again.

## Can I sell the same piece online and at the bazar?
- **Remove from online stock**: no. The pieces come off your Shopify stock when you mark them as sent, until they're back.
- **Keep showing online**: yes, so the last piece can sell online and at the bazar. Traced won't pack a piece that's at the bazar for an online order: that order waits for another piece at the main warehouse.

## Why didn't my online stock change?
- **Keep showing online**: nothing changes when the stock is sent. Shopify updates usually within a few minutes after the sale.
- **Remove from online stock**: Shopify changes when you press **Mark as sent** — not when you create the bazar or scan pieces out.

See [Online stock: Remove or Keep](/help/bazar-online-stock).

## A seller can't close the day
Only owners and managers can close a day or end the bazar. Sellers can sell, return and exchange. See [Close a bazar day](/help/bazar-close-day).

## A seller lost access or forgot their PIN
There is no PIN reset. Send them a new link from the bazar's **Sellers** card — see [Invite sellers](/help/bazar-invite-sellers).
