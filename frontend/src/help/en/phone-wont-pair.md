Most pairing problems are an expired code, a camera the browser isn't allowed to use, or a pairing that has already ended. Read the message on the phone or tablet and match it below.

## On the tablet

- **No Use phone button.** That screen doesn't take phone scans — Pickups and Lookup don't. Open a scanning screen such as Pick & Pack or Scan returns.
- **This code expired.** The QR code lasts about two minutes. Tap **New code** and scan it straight away.
- **Couldn't make a pairing code.** Check the tablet's internet and try again.
- **Phone link reconnecting…** The tablet lost its connection for a moment. Wait; if it doesn't come back, check the tablet's internet.

## On the phone

- **This code was already used or has expired.** Each QR code works once. On the tablet tap **Use phone** again and scan the new code.
- **Camera access was refused.** Allow the camera for this page in the phone's browser settings, then tap **Start scanning**.
- **This browser can't use the camera** or **The camera couldn't start.** Tap **Start scanning** again. **Tip:** if it still fails, open the QR link in the phone's main browser instead of an in-app browser.
- **Can't reach Traced — check the connection.** Turn on Wi-Fi or mobile data, then tap **Try again**.
- **Disconnected — pairing ended.** The pairing stopped: someone tapped **Unpair**, another phone was paired, the worker switched or signed out, the station locked, or 12 hours passed. Scan a new QR code from the tablet.

## Paired, but scans don't land

- **No scanning screen open on the tablet** — open one on the tablet first.
- **Tablet busy — finish the dialog** — close the window open on the tablet.
- **Not applied — scan again** — the scan didn't reach the screen; scan it again.
- The phone doesn't react to a barcode at all — the camera reads only Code 128 and QR. Use a hardware scanner for that label.
