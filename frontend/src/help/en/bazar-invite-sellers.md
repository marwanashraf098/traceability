On the bazar's **Sellers** card, press **Add seller**, create a link and send it on WhatsApp. The seller opens it on their phone, picks a 4-digit PIN and starts selling.

## Add a seller
1. Open the **Sellers** card: on the Get ready page, on the live bazar's **Sellers** tab, or on the phone from the menu › **Sellers**.
2. Press **Add seller**. Pick someone who already works with you, or **Someone new** and type their name (the phone number is optional — it opens their WhatsApp chat).
3. Press **Create link**, then **Send on WhatsApp** or **Copy link**.

Only workers can be invited. Someone new joins your team as a worker. The bazar must let workers sell: if it doesn't, the card offers **Allow workers to sell at this bazar**.

## About the link
- It works once, for 24 hours. Anyone who has it can sign in as that seller, so send it only to them.
- A new link cancels the one you sent before: only the newest works.
- Once they open it, the seller stays signed in on that phone.

## A seller can't get in
The link was used, expired, or a newer one was sent. Press **Get a new link** (or **New sign-in link** once they have a PIN) on their row and send it again.

There is no PIN reset. A seller who loses access — a new phone, a cleared browser, or a forgotten PIN — needs a new link.

## Remove a seller
Press **Remove** on their row. They are signed out and can't sell at this bazar. Someone you added as new with **Add seller** has their account turned off; a team member you picked stays on your team.

## Shared phone
Several sellers can use one phone: the menu's **Switch seller** asks for the seller's PIN.
