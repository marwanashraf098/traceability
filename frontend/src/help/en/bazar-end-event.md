When selling is over, end the bazar, bring the leftovers back to the main warehouse, mark anything missing as lost, then close the transfer. Every day with money must be closed first.

## 1. End the bazar
On the live bazar's page, open **More** › **End event** and confirm with **End event**. Selling, returns, exchanges and payment changes stop.

If some days aren't closed yet, Traced lists them — press **Close the days**, close them, then end the bazar.

## 2. Bring the leftovers back
At the main warehouse, an owner or manager opens **Bring leftovers back** on the bazar's page, on the computer.
- **Scan leftovers back**: scan each piece. A good piece is back in stock. A piece that was taken back damaged at the bazar comes home still damaged.
- **Bring back by count**: for pieces without labels. Press **Choose product and size**, choose how many came back in good shape, then press **Bring back N pieces**. Pieces with labels are scanned.

## 3. Mark missing pieces lost
Pieces that didn't come back are listed under **Not back yet**. Press **Mark lost** on a piece, or tick several and press **Mark N pieces lost**. They leave your stock. Sold pieces are never marked lost.

## 4. Close the transfer
Press **Close the transfer** once every piece is accounted for: sold, back, damaged or lost. Closing also brings home, still damaged, any damaged pieces that weren't scanned — you don't have to scan them first.

Until the transfer is closed, the Bazar list and the report show **Finish the event**.

## Shopify
- **Remove from online stock**: each good leftover goes back on your Shopify stock when it is scanned back or brought back by count. Damaged and lost pieces don't.
- **Keep showing online**: the leftovers never left your Shopify stock. A piece marked lost comes off Shopify, usually within a few minutes.

Damaged pieces stay damaged in Traced. If one turns out fine, see [Back to good](/help/back-to-good).
