On a scanning screen, tap **Use phone** and point your phone's camera at the QR code. The phone becomes that tablet's scanner on every scanning screen until you unpair it or sign out.

## Pair the phone

1. On the tablet (or computer), open a scanning screen and tap **Use phone** in its header. On a narrow screen it's a small phone icon.
2. Open your phone's camera and point it at the QR code. The code is good for about two minutes; if it runs out, tap **New code**.
3. The phone opens a Traced page that says which worker's station it's connected to. Tap **Start scanning** and allow the camera.
4. The tablet now shows **Phone connected**.

## Scan

Point the camera at a waybill or a piece label. Each scan goes to the scanning screen open on the tablet, and the phone shows the result — **Done** or **Not accepted**. Use **Torch on** in a dark corner.

The camera reads Code 128 and QR barcodes only. If the phone doesn't react to a waybill, its barcode type can't be read by the camera — use a hardware scanner for it.

## Where it works

The **Use phone** button is on: **Pick & Pack** (picking and waybill packing), **Scan returns**, **Stock take**, **Transfers** (scan out and the scan-back), **Receiving** (the **High control · Label & verify** scan and the label check of your first receiving), tagging stock and placing pieces on shelves. **Pickups** and **Lookup** don't take phone scans.

## When the pairing ends

A tablet has one phone, and a worker has one phone. The pairing ends when you tap **Unpair**, pair another phone, the worker switches or signs out, the station locks, or after 12 hours. Then scan the QR code again.

Trouble pairing? See [Phone scanner won't pair](/help/phone-wont-pair).
