On the **Bazar prices** card, set one discount for everything, change single products, and give a price to any product that has none. These prices are only for this bazar — your Shopify prices never change.

## A discount on everything
1. Type the discount in **Everything −** … **%**.
2. Set **round to** (5 by default). Prices are rounded to the nearest step, so a few can go up a little.
3. Press **Apply**.

**Back to Shopify prices** removes the discount. If you press **Apply** with no discount typed, only the rounding applies.

## One product
Type a price in the product's **Bazar price** box. Press **Clear** to go back to the discounted (or Shopify) price.

## Products with no price
Without a discount, a product's bazar price is its Shopify price. A product with no Shopify price shows **No price — can't be sold**: Traced never sells a piece at 0. Use the **No price** filter to find them and type a price.

You can change prices while the bazar is live too, on its **Prices** tab. The bazar's A4 price sheet uses these prices.
