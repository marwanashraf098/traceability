Message us on WhatsApp: [wa.me/201037371204](https://wa.me/201037371204). A real person answers, in Arabic or English.

## Where to find it in Traced

- In the **Help center**, under **Still stuck?**, press **Chat on WhatsApp**.
- If a help article didn't answer your question, the WhatsApp link appears there too.
- While you set up your store, the setup screens have a **Need help?** link to the same chat.

## What to send

To get an answer faster, include:

1. What you were trying to do, and on which screen.
2. The order number, tracking number (AWB) or piece barcode it's about.
3. The exact message you saw, or a screenshot. If an alert is involved, a screenshot of it from **Alerts** helps.
4. Whether it happens every time or happened once.

## Other ways to reach us

You can also email **tracedtechnology@gmail.com**, for example for account or privacy questions.

If you'd like someone to walk you through setup instead, see [Book a setup call](/help/setup-call).
