At the end of each selling day, an owner or manager presses **Close day**, counts the cash, enters the card machine total and confirms InstaPay. Traced shows what should be there and what's short or over.

## Open it
Press **Close day** on the live bazar's page (on a phone, on the bazar's home). It opens the oldest day that is still open. You can also pick a day on the **Days & cash** tab › **Close this day**.

## Count
1. **Cash in the drawer**: count everything, the opening float too, and type it in **Counted in the drawer**. **Should be** is the opening float plus the day's cash sales, minus cash given back.
2. **Card machine** (if the bazar takes card): type the total from the machine's settlement slip in **Card machine settlement**.
3. **InstaPay** (if the bazar takes InstaPay): untick **InstaPay received in full** if any InstaPay money hasn't arrived. The whole InstaPay amount then counts as not received.

## Short and over
- **Short**: you counted less than it should be. The missing amount counts as not received. A card shortfall is often the machine's fees.
- **Over**: you counted more than it should be. The extra is kept on the close and shown on its own — it never cancels a shortfall on another method.
- The amount not received is the cash short + the card short + InstaPay not received.

## The drawer
- **Opening float**: the cash the drawer started with. Asked only at the bazar's first close.
- **Kept as change for tomorrow**: what stays in the drawer. Traced shows how much to **take home**, and tomorrow starts with what you kept.

## Close
Press **Close the day**. Selling goes on.

If a sale, return or payment change lands on that day after you closed it, the day shows **Changed after close — close again**. Press **Close again**: the newest close replaces the old one. Sellers can change how a sale was paid only until its day is closed.

## Who can close
Only owners and managers, on the computer or the phone. Sellers can't close a day. Every day with money must be closed before you can end the bazar.
