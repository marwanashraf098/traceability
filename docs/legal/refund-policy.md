# Refund Policy

**Last updated: 10 September 2026**

Traced provides a subscription-based software service. This policy explains our position on refunds.

## 1. All fees are non-refundable

Subscription fees for Traced are non-refundable. This applies to all monthly charges, whether paid by card or by bank transfer/Instapay. We do not issue refunds or credits for partial billing periods, unused time, or downgrades.

## 2. Free trial

Every new account includes a 7-day free trial with full access to Traced at no charge, so you can evaluate whether the service fits your operation before any payment is taken. Your subscription and first charge begin only after the trial ends. We encourage you to use the trial to make your decision.

## 3. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time. Your access continues until the end of the current paid billing period, after which the subscription ends and you are not charged again. Cancellation does not generate a refund for the current or any prior period.

## 4. Billing errors

If you believe you were charged in error (e.g. a duplicate charge or a charge after cancellation), contact us within 14 days at **tracedtechnology@gmail.com** and we will investigate. Where we confirm a genuine billing error on our side, we will correct it.
