Settings / Returns portal

Returns portal

Refund methods

Customers asking for a refund choose one of these. Traced doesn't send money — you refund them yourself.

With none ticked, customers aren't asked how they want their refund. Their details are visible to owners and managers only and are deleted 30 days after the request is refunded or closed.
  • Checkboxes, not switches: several can be on. Order on the portal follows this order.
  • The card sits under “Allow exchanges” in the first settings card group (max-w-3xl), not in Branding.

  • Shown only for a refund, or an exchange with the refund fallback ticked; skipped entirely when the store has no methods on. Required when shown.
  • Steps: refund 1 Find → 2 Items → 3 Refund method → 4 Almost done (“Step x of 4”; “of 3” when skipped). Exchange: the step follows X2.
