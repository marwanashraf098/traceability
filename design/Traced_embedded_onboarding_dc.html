Embedded onboarding: from App Store install to a connected store

A merchant with no Traced account installs the app from the Shopify App Store. Inside Shopify admin they either create a Traced account, with the store taken from the verified Shopify session and never typed, or connect the store to the account they already have. The frame is an illustration of the Shopify admin; everything inside the app area uses DESIGN.md's light system, consistent with the store-finder mockup.
